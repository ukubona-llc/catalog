Before any clinical twin can be named, before any scale from 1 to 5 is chosen, there is a prior question: what is the shape of the space being navigated? Scale 0 is the method layer — cross-cutting, scale-invariant, the epistemological substrate on which every twin in this catalog sits.

The five method artifacts are not arbitrary — they unfold in a deliberate sequence that mirrors the pentad itself. Read the order: it is the fractal.

The method sequence is the TMVES chain applied to itself. Each method answers the question the previous one opens. geo names the territory; tmves compresses it into a navigable coordinate; sib confronts the wall inside that coordinate; eta governs movement through it; gap inherits what is already pre-computed. Territory → Perspective → Wall → Movement → Inheritance. Scale 0 folded back on Scale 0.

01 · The Schema

A name that knows where it lives in the inference chain

Every digital twin in the Ukubona manifold carries its epistemological position in its name. The identifier is not a label — it is a coordinate in a five-dimensional inference space: scale, domain, and crucially, which node of the Bayesian pentad this artifact primarily inhabits.

ukb - {scale} - {domain} - {tmves} - dt - v{nn}
scale 0–5 · Method → Civilization
domain ICD-10 root, ontology key, or method key
tmves t · m · v · e · s
version v00, v01 … sequential

The domain slot uses ICD-10 chapter/block codes at scales 2–3 (Network, System), anatomical shorthand at scale 1 (Person), ISO 3166 country codes at scale 5 (Civilization), and short method keys at scale 0 (Method). Three existing standards plus one new layer. No reinvention beyond what the architecture requires.
