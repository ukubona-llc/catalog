00 · The Method Layer

Scale 0 — the geometry before the content

Before any clinical twin can be named, before any scale from 1 to 5 is chosen, there is a prior question: what is the shape of the space being navigated? Scale 0 is the method layer — cross-cutting, scale-invariant, the epistemological substrate on which every twin in this catalog sits.

Method artifacts do not describe a patient, a network, or a civilization. They describe the rules of inference that apply to all of them equally. The geodesic is the same whether you are walking through a 29-year-old's diagnostic landscape or through 47 years of Middle East geopolitics. The learning rate doctrine is the same whether the irreversible step is a mastectomy or a military strike. Scale 0 is where those invariants live.

Scale 0 is the only scale with a shared URL stem. All method artifacts live at ukb-0-method-{key}-dt-v{nn} — differentiated only by the domain key. The geometry is invariant. The content changes. The address stays home.

The patient is θ ∈ 𝓜 — a situated point on a curved historical surface. The clinician's task is to find the path of least curvature-weighted distance through the diagnostic landscape. That path is the geodesic. SGD is one algorithm for approximating it. The geodesic is what it approximates.

The pentadic Bayesian chain
T → M → V → E → S as P(θ_t) → P(D|θ_t) → P(D) → P(θ|D) → P(θ_{t+1}). The five-node architecture that every twin inhabits. The chain is the method. The node code in the identifier declares current position.
eta · irreversibility doctrine
ukb-0-method-eta-dt-v00
The catastrophic learning rate
η must be proportional to reversibility. An irreversible step at incomplete evidence is η = ∞ on an unmapped landscape. The gate condition: max(L) > 0.85, tissue confirmation obtained, N_completeness > 0.90. The S-node does not fire before the E-node resolves.
sib · sovereignty tool
ukb-0-method-sib-dt-v00
Sovereign Intelligence Broker
The V-node tool. Where data is locked, feared, or siloed, the SIB treats inaccessible data as a constrained prior rather than a missing input. Missing data = wide prior, not a dead end. The wall constrains the posterior — it does not kill inference.
gap · inherited scalars
ukb-0-method-gap-dt-v00
Grey Aware Policy
Some geodesics are already known. When the manifold is so well-mapped at the population level that the path is pre-computed — tPA yes/no, MUAC thresholds, ABCDE trauma chain — the individual twin inherits the scalar rather than re-running the full M→V→E sequence. Grey Aware Policy is the doctrine governing when that inheritance is valid and when it must yield to individual curvature. It is the fast path. The geodesic is the slow path. Both are necessary.

The organ at Kings College Budo was computing geodesics on an acoustic manifold. Psalm 23 named the master geodesic before the vocabulary existed. ℝⁿ is 𝓜 with history assumed away — the special case, not the general one.

01 · The Schema

A name that knows where it lives in the inference chain

Every digital twin in the Ukubona manifold carries its epistemological position in its name. The identifier is not a label — it is a coordinate in a five-dimensional inference space: scale, domain, and crucially, which node of the Bayesian pentad this artifact primarily inhabits.

ukb - {scale} - {domain} - {tmves} - dt - v{nn}
scale 0–5 · Method → Civilization
domain ICD-10 root, ontology key, or method key
tmves t · m · v · e · s
version v00, v01 … sequential

The domain slot uses ICD-10 chapter/block codes at scales 2–3 (Network, System), anatomical shorthand at scale 1 (Person), ISO 3166 country codes at scale 5 (Civilization), and short method keys at scale 0 (Method). Three existing standards plus one new layer. No reinvention beyond what the architecture requires.

02 · The Pentad

T → M → V → E → S   /   P(θ_t) → P(D|θ_t) → P(D) → P(θ|D) → P(θ_{t+1})

The five TMVES nodes map exactly onto the Bayesian inference chain. The twin's TMVES code declares which node it primarily inhabits — where its intelligence lives, and where it applies leverage on the clinical decision.

T
Tensor
P(θ_t)
The accumulated prior. Civilization, population, policy.
M
Matrix
P(D|θ_t)
Study design. What data would you expect, given that prior?
V
Vector
P(D)
The data itself. The governance wall. Sovereignty lives here.
E
Eigenmode
P(θ|D)
The posterior. Identity updated by evidence.
S
Scalar
P(θ_{t+1})
The decision. The loop closes. Prior updates.

T and S are Grey Aware Policy. The prior (T) is set by population science, civilization-scale data, clinical guidelines — sparse in the twin catalog because it is inherited, not computed fresh. The decision (S) is the clinical scalar: tPA yes/no, refer vs operate. Once E is good, S is often a one-liner. The intelligence lives in M, V, and E. The governance failure lives at V.

V is where medicine keeps failing. HIPAA, GDPR, stigma, siloed labs, institutional fear — these are not data problems. They are V-node sovereignty failures. The decentralized Sovereign Intelligence Broker is specifically a V-node tool. Missing data = wide prior, not a dead end. The wall constrains the posterior; it does not kill inference.

Decentralized sovereignty at the V node

The Sovereign Intelligence Broker is not a product. It is a position in the inference chain. Every twin in this catalog is a rehearsal of the same fundamental act: conditioning on everything known about a person, then updating when new evidence arrives.

The naming convention is the infrastructure of that act. A twin without a name that encodes its epistemic position is a model without a coordinate. The manifold has no address. The inference cannot be routed.
